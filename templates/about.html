WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Winners Chess Academy

Winners Chess Academy is a chess training academy dedicated to helping players discover, develop and improve their chess abilities.

We believe that chess is more than a game. It teaches patience, discipline, calculation, strategic thinking and decision-making.

Our goal is to create an environment where players can learn, practice, compete and grow together.
